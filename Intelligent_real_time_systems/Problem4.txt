4.1 — Scheduling scheme
A scheme with two parts: (1) an algorithm for ordering the use of system resources (mainly the CPU), and (2) a means of predicting the worst-case behaviour when that algorithm is applied. The prediction is what lets you confirm the application's temporal requirements.

4.2 — Static vs dynamic
Static: predictions/decisions made before execution (offline). Dynamic: decisions made at run-time. The course focuses on static schemes.

4.3 ii) Minor and major cycle
Minor cycle: the fixed interval between timer interrupts, i.e. one frame. Equals the GCD of the periods (25 above). All periods must be a multiple of it.
Major cycle: the full schedule that repeats. Equals the LCM of the periods (100 above), and is the longest period that can be handled without secondary schedules.
